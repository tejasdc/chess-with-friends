chess with friends — icon candidates

Mockup palette (celadon ground, buff+teal-dark board, navy ink, cream chip). No vermillion. Each shown at 180×180 (retina apple-touch-icon) and 60×60 (approx iPhone home-screen render). iOS applies its own rounded-mask; the preview here uses 22% radius to approximate.

A
Bare 2×2 board — pure Bauhaus geometry, no pieces, no accent. Reads as chess through the alternating pattern. Very quiet on a home screen; may be under-specified vs the noise of neighbouring icons. Safe.
B
The whole back rank — 8 tiny cream silhouettes on a teal-dark shelf against the celadon ground. Reads as "chess set at rest". Sidesteps the chess.com pawn / lichess knight problem by showing the entire ensemble rather than any hero piece. Loses detail at 60px but keeps the shape.
C
Two chairs facing each other, board between — the Rodchenko thesis mark. Cream on navy, no piece iconography at all. Says "sit down with a friend and play" in one glyph. Distinct from any competitor in the space. At 60px the chairs likely simplify to two vertical strokes with a tiny cream tag between — still readable as pairing.
D
Close-up board + move dot — a 3×3 corner-crop of a real board (edge-to-edge, no framing), with a single deep-ink dot marking a legal move on the center square. Says "chess app you make moves on" with the exact visual language the game screen uses. Board is the object; the dot is the interaction.
